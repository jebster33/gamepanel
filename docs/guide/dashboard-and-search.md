# Dashboard, search and organising servers

These are personal settings: they are saved on your account, follow you to any browser, and never change what anyone else sees.

**Search and commands (Ctrl+K or Cmd+K).** Press it anywhere, or click the magnifier in the top bar. Type a few letters (they do not have to be next to each other: "rstm" finds "Rust main") to find servers, pages, settings sections, game templates, players (on servers where you have console access) and actions such as start, stop, restart, open console and back up now. Move with the arrow keys, press Enter to run the highlighted item, Esc to close. With nothing typed you see what you picked recently. You only ever see servers, pages and actions your account is allowed to use. Stop and restart ask for confirmation.

**Pinning.** Click the star on a server to pin it. Pinned servers appear in their own **Pinned** section at the top of the dashboard, first in the Servers list and first in the sidebar.

**Folders and tags.** On the Servers page or the dashboard, use the tag button on a server to give it tags (for example `pvp, modded`) and put it in a folder. **New folder** creates one. Click a folder's name to collapse it. Folders have start and stop buttons for everything in them that your account may power. Tag chips above the list show only servers with that tag. Deleting a folder never touches its servers.

**Customising the dashboard.** Press **Customize** above the dashboard sections. Each section gets **Move up**, **Move down** and **Hide/Show** buttons (drag and drop also works with a mouse), so it can all be done from the keyboard. **Reset layout** restores the default. Press **Done** when finished.
